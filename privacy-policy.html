Privacy Policy

Quest Map is a location-based challenge game for Hamilton, Scotland, published by C85 Games ("we", "us"), an individual developer based in the UK. The short version: Quest Map has no accounts and no servers. Everything you put into it stays on your phone.

Who to contact

C85 Games, email brianwands656@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

What the app stores, and where

All of the following is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Your name or nickname, shown on your profile.
  • Location readings. While the app is open, Quest Map uses your device's location (precise and approximate) to show your position on the map, measure your distance to a stop and confirm check-ins within 50 metres. It also notes whether your device reports a simulated ("mock") location, for fair play. Location is only used while the app is open, never in the background.
  • Photos you take for photo challenges with the in-app camera. They are saved in the app's own folder, not your gallery, and are not uploaded.
  • Game progress: quests played, check-ins, scores, XP, level and settings.

We never receive this information, so we cannot see, sell or share it.

Permissions

  • Location (while using the app): to show your position and confirm check-ins. You can refuse it, or use Practice mode, which simulates your position.
  • Camera: only for photo challenges.
  • Internet: only to download the map.
  • Vibration: for check-in feedback.

The app does not use your microphone, your photo gallery or background location.

The map (OpenFreeMap)

The map is downloaded from OpenFreeMap, like a web page loading pictures. As with any web request, OpenFreeMap receives your device's IP address and which area of the map is being loaded. Because the map follows your position, this can indicate roughly where you are. We do not receive this information. OpenFreeMap says it does not keep IP addresses by default and sets no cookies: see openfreemap.org/privacy. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

What we do not do

No accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, no in-app purchases, and no sharing or selling of personal data.

Google Play

You download Quest Map through Google Play, which is run by Google under its own privacy policy. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google may give us anonymous, aggregated crash and performance reports that do not identify you.

Testers: if you join a Quest Map test, we hold the email address you gave us so Google Play can give you access, and any feedback you email us. We use these only to run the test, and delete them when you ask or when testing ends.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. To delete everything, open Me › Erase all data in the app, or uninstall it. Both remove your profile, progress and photos. Because we never hold a copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side, apart from tester emails and feedback described above.

Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. The map is downloaded over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Anyone who can unlock your phone could open the app and see your progress and photos.

Age

Quest Map is for adults aged 18 and over, because some quests visit pubs and bars. It is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

Because we hold no personal data from the app, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete: you control your data directly on your device. If you are a tester or have emailed us, you can ask us for a copy or deletion of that information. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

If the app changes (for example, if a future version adds online accounts or leaderboards), we will update this policy before that version is released, and change the date below.

Effective date: 28 September 2026.
